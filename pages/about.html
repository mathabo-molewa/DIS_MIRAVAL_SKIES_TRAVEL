Company Background

Miraval Skies Travel is a South African luxury travel agency connecting travellers with their dream destinations. Founded in 2023 by Mathabo Molewa, the company later welcomed a team of travel professionals to help make travel dreams a reality. Initially focused on corporate clients, the company has expanded its services to include leisure travel. Miraval Skies Travel is committed to providing exceptional customer service and memorable luxury travel experiences while continuing to grow its services to meet clients' needs.

Who Are We?

  • Travel Specialists: Our team is experienced in different aspects of the travel industry and is committed to providing excellent service.
  • Client-Focused: We focus on understanding clients' requests and creating memorable luxury travel experiences.
  • Experience Creators: We help clients plan journeys to the destinations they have always dreamed of visiting.

What Do We Believe In?

  • Quality travel experiences.
  • Customer satisfaction.
  • Creating unforgettable travel memories.
