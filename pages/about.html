Company Background

Miraval Skies Travel is a South African-based luxury travel agency connecting tourists with their dream destination. It was founded in 2023 by Mathabo Molewa, and later a team of travel experts joined the company to help make travel dreams a reality. The company began by providing travel services to corporate client, but has since opened a wider horizon to include leisure travel. The company has a strong focus on providing out of this worl luxury travel experiences and will continue to expand its services to meet the needs of its clients. The company is committed to providing exceptional customer service and ensuring that every client has a memorable travel experience.
